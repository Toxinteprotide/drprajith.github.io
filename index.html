Professional summary

Bridging clinical evidence, pharmacology and medical education

My work sits where drug delivery science meets medical affairs. I author non-promotional medical information, run systematic evidence syntheses, curate open pharmacokinetic data, and build user-centric platforms that make complex science usable for clinicians, researchers and regulators.

The through-line is access: long-acting medicines, open data and clear scientific communication that accelerate patient care, particularly in pediatric and global health settings.

  • Long-acting therapeutics and drug delivery platforms
  • Clinical pharmacology and pharmacokinetic data sharing
  • Compliance-aligned writing (GPP 2022, ABPI)
  • KOL engagement and scientific exchange
